Agent leverage and agent runtime answer different questions. These published numbers describe specific groups and periods. Neither establishes your productivity or an individual percentile.

Keep the denominator in view.

Agent-hours / human hour

A runtime ratio. Human hours and agent hours must cover the same period. Weekly human hours in our calculator are an explicit estimate.

Agent-hours / active day

A daily runtime statistic for active users. Its denominator differs from our score’s 30 calendar days, which include zero-use days.
