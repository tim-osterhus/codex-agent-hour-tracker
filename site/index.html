Stats for Nerds

What counts?

We add up completed Codex turns over the last 30 full days, ending yesterday. If three agents each run for an hour, that counts as three agent hours. Days with no use still count in the average.

Which sessions?

The default counts interactive sessions. Add --include-exec to count batch sessions too. These scores have their own community list.

How does the math work?

Daily hours = total hours ÷ 30. The preview multiplies agents by hours per agent. The optional ratio divides agent hours by your estimated time: weekly hours × 30 ÷ 7.

What about gaps and missing logs?

Each gap between recorded events counts for up to 30 minutes. Sparse logs use a short fallback estimate. Unfinished turns do not count. Missing logs can lower your score.

Which day gets the hours?

A turn's full time counts on the day it started, in your chosen timezone. We count matching turn IDs only once when you combine profiles or machines.
